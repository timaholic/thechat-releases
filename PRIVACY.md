# Gainware Chat — Privacy Policy

*Effective: July 24, 2026*

Gainware Chat is a voice-to-text intercom for live production crews. It is built to keep everything on your own devices and your own local network.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Gainware Chat has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours that your data could reach. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

## How the app works with your data

- **Your voice** is transcribed into text **on your device** using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio never leaves the device that captured it.
- **Messages, photos, names, and profile pictures** travel only over your **local network**, directly between the devices in your session (and any web browser on the same network that you invite via the session's QR code). They are not uploaded to the internet.
- **Session history** lives in the memory of the devices in the session and is gone when the session ends. There is no cloud copy.
- **Microphone access** is used solely for the transcription described above, and only while you have the microphone enabled. An optional setting ("Pause mic in background") stops all audio capture whenever the app is not on screen.
- **Camera and photo access** are used solely to let you take or choose a profile photo or send a photo into the session.
- **Local network and Bonjour access** are used solely to discover and connect to sessions on your network.
- **Notifications** are generated locally on your device when messages arrive. No push-notification service is involved.

## The Mac app

The Mac version sold directly by Gainware validates its license key with our sales platform (Polar.sh) when you activate it. That check sends the license key you typed and nothing else. The iOS and watchOS apps have no license system and make no such request.

## Changes

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated here first, and the change will be described in the release notes of the version that introduces it.
